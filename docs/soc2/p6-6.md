# SOC2 - P6.6
## Principle
The entity provides notification of breaches and incidents to affected data subjects, regulators, and others to meet the entity's objectives related to privacy.
## Points of Focus
### P6.6.a: Identifies Reporting Requirements [P][C]
Determines breach notification requirements.
### P6.6.b: Provides Notice of Breaches and Incidents [P][C]
Implements notification process.
