# SOC2 - P5.1
## Principle
The entity grants identified and authenticated data subjects the ability to access their stored personal information for review and, upon request, provides physical or electronic copies of that information to data subjects to meet the entity's objectives related to privacy. If access is denied, data subjects are informed of the denial and reason for such denial, as required, to meet the entity's objectives related to privacy.
## Points of Focus
### P5.1.a: Responds to Data Controller Requests [P]
Processes access requests from controllers.
### P5.1.b: Authenticates Data Subjects' Identity [P][C]
Verifies identity before granting access.
### P5.1.c: Permits Data Subjects Access to Their Personal Information [P][C]
Allows access to stored information.
### P5.1.d: Provides Understandable Personal Information Within Reasonable Time [P][C]
Delivers information in usable form.
### P5.1.e: Informs Data Subjects If Access Is Denied [P][C]
Explains access denial reasons.
