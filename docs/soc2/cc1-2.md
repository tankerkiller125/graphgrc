# SOC2 - CC1.2
## Principle
COSO Principle 2: The board of directors demonstrates independence from management and exercises oversight of internal control development and performance.
## Points of Focus
### CC1.2.a: Establishes Oversight Responsibilities
The board identifies and accepts oversight responsibilities.
### CC1.2.b: Applies Relevant Expertise
The board defines and evaluates skills and expertise needed among its members.
### CC1.2.c: Operates Independently
The board has sufficient independent members for objective evaluations.
### CC1.2.d: Supplements Board Expertise
The board supplements expertise in security, availability, processing integrity, confidentiality, and privacy as needed.
