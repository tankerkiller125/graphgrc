# SOC2 - P4.3
## Principle
The entity securely disposes of personal information to meet the entity's objectives related to privacy.
## Points of Focus
### P4.3.a: Captures, Identifies, and Flags Requests for Deletion [P][C]
Tracks deletion requests.
### P4.3.b: Disposes of, Destroys, and Redacts Personal Information [P][C]
Properly eliminates information.
### P4.3.c: Destroys Personal Information [P][C]
Implements destruction procedures.
