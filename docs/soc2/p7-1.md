# SOC2 - P7.1
## Principle
The entity collects and maintains accurate, up-to-date, complete, and relevant personal information to meet the entity's objectives related to privacy.
## Points of Focus
### P7.1.a: Ensures Accuracy and Completeness of Personal Information [P][C]
Maintains accurate information.
### P7.1.b: Ensures Relevance of Personal Information [P][C]
Ensures information is relevant for intended use.
