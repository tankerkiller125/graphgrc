# SOC2 - CC1.1
## Principle
COSO Principle 1: The entity demonstrates a commitment to integrity and ethical values.
## Points of Focus
### CC1.1.a: Sets the Tone at the Top
The board of directors and management demonstrate the importance of integrity and ethical values.
### CC1.1.b: Establishes Standards of Conduct
The expectations are defined in standards of conduct and understood at all levels.
### CC1.1.c: Evaluates Adherence to Standards of Conduct
Processes evaluate individuals and teams against expected standards.
### CC1.1.d: Addresses Deviations in a Timely Manner
Deviations are identified and remedied in a timely and consistent manner.
### CC1.1.e: Considers Contractors and Vendor Employees in Demonstrating Its Commitment
Includes contractors and vendors in standards of conduct processes.
