# SOC2 - CC1.3
## Principle
COSO Principle 3: Management establishes, with board oversight, structures, reporting lines, and appropriate authorities and responsibilities in the pursuit of objectives.
## Points of Focus
### CC1.3.a: Considers All Structures of the Entity
Management considers multiple structures used to support objectives.
### CC1.3.b: Establishes Reporting Lines
Management designs lines of reporting to enable execution of authorities.
### CC1.3.c: Defines, Assigns, and Limits Authorities and Responsibilities
Management delegates authority and segregates duties.
### CC1.3.d: Addresses Specific Requirements When Defining Authorities and Responsibilities
Considers requirements for security, availability, processing integrity, confidentiality, and privacy.
### CC1.3.e: Considers Interactions With External Parties
Considers interactions with and monitoring of external parties.
### CC1.3.f: Establishes Structures, Reporting Lines, and Authorities to Support Compliance With Legal and Contractual Privacy Requirements
For privacy-related matters.
