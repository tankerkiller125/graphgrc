# SOC2 - CC7.3
## Principle
The entity evaluates security events to determine whether they could or have resulted in a failure of the entity to meet its objectives (security incidents) and, if so, takes actions to prevent or address such failures.
## Points of Focus
### CC7.3.a: Responds to Security Incidents
Has incident response procedures.
### CC7.3.b: Communicates and Reviews Detected Security Events
Reports events to security management.
### CC7.3.c: Develops and Implements Procedures to Analyze Security Incidents
Analyzes impact of incidents.
### CC7.3.d: Assesses the Impact on Confidential Information
Evaluates unauthorized disclosure of confidential information.
### CC7.3.e: Determines Confidential Information Used or Disclosed
Identifies affected information and addresses control failures.
### CC7.3.f: Assesses the Impact on Personal Information
Evaluates unauthorized disclosure of personal information.
### CC7.3.g: Determines Personal Information Used or Disclosed
Identifies affected personal information and addresses failures.
