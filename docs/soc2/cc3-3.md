# SOC2 - CC3.3
## Principle
COSO Principle 8: The entity considers the potential for fraud in assessing risks to the achievement of objectives.
## Points of Focus
### CC3.3.a: Considers Various Types of Fraud
Considers fraudulent reporting, asset loss, and corruption.
### CC3.3.b: Assesses Incentives and Pressures
Considers incentives and pressures for fraud.
### CC3.3.c: Assesses Opportunities
Evaluates opportunities for inappropriate acts.
### CC3.3.d: Assesses Attitudes and Rationalizations
Considers how personnel might justify inappropriate actions.
### CC3.3.e: Considers the Risks Related to the Use of IT and Access to Information
Evaluates IT-specific fraud risks.
