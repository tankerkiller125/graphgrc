# SOC2 - CC9.2
## Principle
The entity assesses and manages risks associated with vendors and business partners.
## Points of Focus
### CC9.2.a: Establishes Requirements for Vendor and Business Partner Engagements
Defines service and compliance requirements.
### CC9.2.b: Identifies Vulnerabilities
Evaluates risks from third-party system access.
### CC9.2.c: Assesses Vendor and Business Partner Risks
Evaluates threats from external relationships.
### CC9.2.d: Assigns Responsibility and Accountability for Managing Vendors and Business Partners
Designates oversight responsibilities.
### CC9.2.e: Establishes Communication Protocols for Vendors and Business Partners
Creates resolution procedures.
### CC9.2.f: Establishes Exception Handling Procedures From Vendors and Business Partners
Addresses service issues.
### CC9.2.g: Assesses Vendor and Business Partner Performance
Evaluates performance based on risk.
### CC9.2.h: Implements Procedures for Addressing Issues Identified During Vendor and Business Partner Assessments
Addresses relationship issues.
### CC9.2.i: Implements Procedures for Terminating Vendor and Business Partner Relationships
Includes data return procedures.
### CC9.2.j: Obtains Confidentiality Commitments From Vendors and Business Partners
Secures commitments for confidential information.
### CC9.2.k: Assesses Compliance With Confidentiality Commitments of Vendors and Business Partners
Monitors compliance.
### CC9.2.l: Obtains Privacy Commitments From Vendors and Business Partners
Secures commitments for personal information.
### CC9.2.m: Assesses Compliance With Privacy Commitments of Vendors and Business Partners
Monitors compliance.
