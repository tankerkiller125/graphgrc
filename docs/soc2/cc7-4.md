# SOC2 - CC7.4
## Principle
The entity responds to identified security incidents by executing a defined incident-response program to understand, contain, remediate, and communicate security incidents, as appropriate.
## Points of Focus
### CC7.4.a: Assigns Roles and Responsibilities
Designates roles for incident response.
### CC7.4.b: Contains and Responds to Security Incidents
Responds to active security threats.
### CC7.4.c: Mitigates Ongoing Security Incidents
Reduces effects of ongoing incidents.
### CC7.4.d: Resolves Security Incidents
Addresses vulnerabilities and removes unauthorized access.
### CC7.4.e: Restores Operations
Restores operations to support objectives.
### CC7.4.f: Develops and Implements Communication of Security Incidents
Creates protocols for communication.
### CC7.4.g: Obtains Understanding of Nature of Incident and Determines Containment Strategy
Analyzes incidents.
### CC7.4.h: Remediates Identified Vulnerabilities
Develops remediation activities.
### CC7.4.i: Communicates Remediation Activities
Documents and communicates remediation actions.
### CC7.4.j: Evaluates the Effectiveness of Incident Response
Periodically assesses response effectiveness.
### CC7.4.k: Periodically Evaluates Incidents
Reviews incidents to identify needed system changes.
### CC7.4.l: Applies Breach Response Procedures
Implements defined procedures for privacy incidents.
### CC7.4.m: Communicates Unauthorized Use and Disclosure
Informs required parties of privacy breaches.
### CC7.4.n: Application of Sanctions
Evaluates conduct of involved parties and applies sanctions as needed.
