# SOC2 - CC3.1
## Principle
COSO Principle 6: The entity specifies objectives with sufficient clarity to enable the identification and assessment of risks relating to objectives.
## Points of Focus
### CC3.1.a: Operations Objectives
Reflect management choices, consider risk tolerances, include performance goals, form basis for resource commitment.
### CC3.1.b: External Financial Reporting Objectives
Comply with accounting standards, consider materiality, reflect entity activities.
### CC3.1.c: External Nonfinancial Reporting Objectives
Comply with external frameworks, consider precision requirements, reflect entity activities.
### CC3.1.d: Internal Reporting Objectives
Reflect management choices, consider precision requirements, reflect entity activities.
### CC3.1.e: Compliance Objectives
Reflect external laws and regulations, consider risk tolerances.
### CC3.1.f: Establishes Sub-Objectives for Risk Assessment
Identifies sub-objectives for security, availability, processing integrity, confidentiality, or privacy.
