# SOC2 - CC6.4
## Principle
The entity restricts physical access to facilities and protected information assets (for example, data center facilities, backup media storage, and other sensitive locations) to authorized personnel to meet the entity's objectives.
## Points of Focus
### CC6.4.a: Creates or Modifies Physical Access
Processes for authorizing physical access.
### CC6.4.b: Removes Physical Access
Processes for removing unnecessary physical access.
### CC6.4.c: Recovers Physical Devices
Processes for recovering entity devices when access is terminated.
### CC6.4.d: Reviews Physical Access
Periodically reviews physical access for appropriateness.
