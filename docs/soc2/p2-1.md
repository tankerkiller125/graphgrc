# SOC2 - P2.1
## Principle
The entity communicates choices available regarding the collection, use, retention, disclosure, and disposal of personal information to the data subjects and the consequences, if any, of each choice. Explicit consent for the collection, use, retention, disclosure, and disposal of personal information is obtained from data subjects or other authorized persons, if required. Such consent is obtained only for the intended purpose of the information to meet the entity's objectives related to privacy. The entity's basis for determining implicit consent for the collection, use, retention, disclosure, and disposal of personal information is documented.
## Points of Focus
### P2.1.a: Communicates to Data Subjects [C]
Informs about collection choices and consent requirements.
### P2.1.b: Communicates Consequences of Denying or Withdrawing Consent [C]
Explains consequences of consent decisions.
### P2.1.c: Obtains Implicit or Explicit Consent [C]
Gets consent at collection time and implements preferences.
### P2.1.d: Documents and Obtains Consent for New Purposes and Uses [C]
Gets consent for new uses of information.
### P2.1.e: Obtains Explicit Consent for Sensitive Information [C]
Gets explicit consent for sensitive data.
### P2.1.f: Obtains Consent for Data Transfers [C]
Gets consent before transferring to/from endpoint devices.
