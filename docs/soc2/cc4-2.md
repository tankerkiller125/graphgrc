# SOC2 - CC4.2
## Principle
COSO Principle 17: The entity evaluates and communicates internal control deficiencies in a timely manner to those parties responsible for taking corrective action, including senior management and the board of directors, as appropriate.
## Points of Focus
### CC4.2.a: Assesses Results
Management and the board assess evaluation results.
### CC4.2.b: Communicates Deficiencies
Reports deficiencies to responsible parties.
### CC4.2.c: Monitors Corrective Action
Tracks timely remediation of deficiencies.
