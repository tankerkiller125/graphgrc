# SOC2 - P6.7
## Principle
The entity provides data subjects with an accounting of the personal information held and disclosure of the data subjects' personal information, upon the data subjects' request, to meet the entity's objectives related to privacy.
## Points of Focus
### P6.7.a: Responds to Data Controller Requests [P]
Processes accounting requests.
### P6.7.b: Identifies Types of Personal Information and Handling Process [P][C]
Identifies information types and processes.
### P6.7.c: Captures, Identifies, and Communicates Requests for Information [P][C]
Processes information requests.
