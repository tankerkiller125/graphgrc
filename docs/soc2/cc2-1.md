# SOC2 - CC2.1
## Principle
COSO Principle 13: The entity obtains or generates and uses relevant, quality information to support the functioning of internal control.
## Points of Focus
### CC2.1.a: Identifies Information Requirements
Identifies information needed to support internal control components.
### CC2.1.b: Captures Internal and External Sources of Data
Information systems capture data sources.
### CC2.1.c: Processes Relevant Data Into Information
Systems transform data into information.
### CC2.1.d: Maintains Quality Throughout Processing
Systems produce timely, accurate, accessible, and protected information.
### CC2.1.e: Documents Data Flow
Documents and uses data flows to support controls.
### CC2.1.f: Manages Assets
Identifies, documents, and maintains records of system components.
### CC2.1.g: Classifies Information
Classifies information by relevant characteristics to support control design.
### CC2.1.h: Uses Information That Is Complete and Accurate
Uses valid information in control operation.
### CC2.1.i: Manages the Location of Assets
Tracks physical location of information assets, especially those stored externally.
