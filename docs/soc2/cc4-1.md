# SOC2 - CC4.1
## Principle
COSO Principle 16: The entity selects, develops, and performs ongoing and/or separate evaluations to ascertain whether the components of internal control are present and functioning.
## Points of Focus
### CC4.1.a: Considers a Mix of Ongoing and Separate Evaluations
Balances ongoing and separate evaluations.
### CC4.1.b: Considers Rate of Change
Considers business change rate when developing evaluations.
### CC4.1.c: Establishes Baseline Understanding
Uses system design as evaluation baseline.
### CC4.1.d: Uses Knowledgeable Personnel
Ensures evaluators understand what they're evaluating.
### CC4.1.e: Integrates With Business Processes
Builds ongoing evaluations into business processes.
### CC4.1.f: Adjusts Scope and Frequency
Varies evaluation scope based on risk.
### CC4.1.g: Objectively Evaluates
Performs periodic separate evaluations for objective feedback.
### CC4.1.h: Considers Different Types of Ongoing and Separate Evaluations
Uses various monitoring methods.
