# SOC2 - CC3.4
## Principle
COSO Principle 9: The entity identifies and assesses changes that could significantly impact the system of internal control.
## Points of Focus
### CC3.4.a: Assesses Changes in the External Environment
Considers regulatory, economic, and physical environment changes.
### CC3.4.b: Assesses Changes in the Business Model
Considers impacts of business changes on internal control.
### CC3.4.c: Assesses Changes in Leadership
Considers changes in management attitudes toward internal control.
### CC3.4.d: Assesses Changes in Systems and Technology
Considers impacts of technology changes.
### CC3.4.e: Assesses Changes in Vendor and Business Partner Relationships
Considers impacts of relationship changes.
### CC3.4.f: Assesses Changes in Threats and Vulnerabilities
Evaluates changing threats to system components.
