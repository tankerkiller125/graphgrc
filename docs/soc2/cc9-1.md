# SOC2 - CC9.1
## Principle
The entity identifies, selects, and develops risk mitigation activities for risks arising from potential business disruptions.
## Points of Focus
### CC9.1.a: Considers Mitigation of Risks of Business Disruption
Develops resilience policies and procedures.
### CC9.1.b: Considers the Use of Insurance to Mitigate Financial Impact Risks
Evaluates insurance options.
