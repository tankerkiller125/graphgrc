# SOC2 - CC3.2
## Principle
COSO Principle 7: The entity identifies risks to the achievement of its objectives across the entity and analyzes risks as a basis for determining how the risks should be managed.
## Points of Focus
### CC3.2.a: Includes Entity, Subsidiary, Division, Operating Unit, and Functional Levels
Identifies risks at all relevant levels.
### CC3.2.b: Analyzes Internal and External Factors
Considers impact of factors on objectives.
### CC3.2.c: Involves Appropriate Levels of Management
Includes appropriate management in risk assessment.
### CC3.2.d: Estimates Significance of Risks Identified
Analyzes potential significance of risks.
### CC3.2.e: Determines How to Respond to Risks
Decides whether to accept, avoid, reduce, or share risks.
### CC3.2.f: Identifies Threats to Objectives
Identifies threats from intentional and unintentional acts and events.
### CC3.2.g: Identifies Vulnerability of System Components
Identifies vulnerabilities in infrastructure, software, and assets.
### CC3.2.h: Analyzes Threats and Vulnerabilities From Vendors, Business Partners, and Other Parties
Considers external threats.
### CC3.2.i: Assesses the Significance of the Risks
Analyzes criticality, susceptibility, likelihood, magnitude, and develops mitigations.
