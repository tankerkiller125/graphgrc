# SOC2 - P3.1
## Principle
Personal information is collected consistent with the entity's objectives related to privacy.
## Points of Focus
### P3.1.a: Limits the Collection of Personal Information [P][C]
Collects only necessary information.
### P3.1.b: Collects Information by Fair and Lawful Means [P][C]
Reviews collection methods for fairness and legality.
### P3.1.c: Collects Information From Reliable Sources [P][C]
Verifies reliability of third-party data sources.
### P3.1.d: Informs Data Subjects When Additional Information Is Acquired [P][C]
Communicates about additional information acquisition.
