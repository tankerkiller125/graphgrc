# SOC2 - C1.1
## Principle
The entity identifies and maintains confidential information to meet the entity's objectives related to confidentiality.
## Points of Focus
### C1.1.a: Defines and Identifies Confidential information
Designates confidential information when received or created.
### C1.1.b: Retains Confidential Information
Retains only as long as necessary for purpose.
### C1.1.c: Protects Confidential Information From Destruction
Prevents erasure during retention period.
