# SOC2 - CC6.7
## Principle
The entity restricts the transmission, movement, and removal of information to authorized internal and external users and processes, and protects it during transmission, movement, or removal to meet the entity's objectives.
## Points of Focus
### CC6.7.a: Restricts the Ability to Perform Transmission
Uses data loss prevention to control data movement.
### CC6.7.b: Uses Encryption Technologies or Secure Communication Channels to Protect Data
Protects data during transmission.
### CC6.7.c: Protects Removal Media
Secures removable media through encryption and physical protections.
### CC6.7.d: Protects Endpoint Devices
Implements controls for mobile devices, laptops, and other endpoints.
