# SOC2 - C1.2
## Principle
The entity disposes of confidential information to meet the entity's objectives related to confidentiality.
## Points of Focus
### C1.2.a: Identifies Confidential Information for Destruction
Identifies information at end of retention period.
### C1.2.b: Destroys Confidential Information
Erases or destroys information identified for destruction.
