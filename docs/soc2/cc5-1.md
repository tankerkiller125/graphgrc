# SOC2 - CC5.1
## Principle
COSO Principle 10: The entity selects and develops control activities that contribute to the mitigation of risks to the achievement of objectives to acceptable levels.
## Points of Focus
### CC5.1.a: Integrates With Risk Assessment
Control activities address and mitigate risks.
### CC5.1.b: Considers Entity-Specific Factors
Considers environment and organizational characteristics in control selection.
### CC5.1.c: Determines Relevant Business Processes
Identifies processes requiring controls.
### CC5.1.d: Evaluates a Mix of Control Activity Types
Uses various control types including manual, automated, preventive, and detective.
### CC5.1.e: Considers at What Level Activities Are Applied
Implements controls at various entity levels.
### CC5.1.f: Addresses Segregation of Duties
Segregates incompatible duties or develops alternative controls.
