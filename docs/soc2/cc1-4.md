# SOC2 - CC1.4
## Principle
COSO Principle 4: The entity demonstrates a commitment to attract, develop, and retain competent individuals in alignment with objectives.
## Points of Focus
### CC1.4.a: Establishes Policies and Practices
Policies reflect necessary competence to support objectives.
### CC1.4.b: Evaluates Competence and Addresses Shortcomings
Evaluates competence across entity and outsourced providers.
### CC1.4.c: Attracts, Develops, and Retains Individuals
Provides mentoring and training to attract and retain personnel.
### CC1.4.d: Plans and Prepares for Succession
Develops contingency plans for key responsibilities.
### CC1.4.e: Considers the Background of Individuals
Evaluates background when determining employment.
### CC1.4.f: Considers the Technical Competency of Individuals
Evaluates technical skills of personnel and vendors.
### CC1.4.g: Provides Training to Maintain Technical Competencies
Provides ongoing education and training.
